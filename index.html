How GSTSyncPro Works

From file upload to complete ITC summary and portal JSON export in four easy steps. 100% private in your browser.

1

Upload Files (2-Files or 4-Files)

In 2-Files Mode, upload GST Portal files directly as-is without editing. In 4-Files Mode, upload separate Purchase & CN/DN files using standard template headers for both Portal and Books files.

2

Smart Auto-Match

GSTSyncPro runs smart auto-matching algorithms across purchases, credit notes, and debit notes with automatic comma cleaning for exact amount precision.

3

Side-by-Side Pairing Review

Review unmatched and pending entries side-by-side in Chrome. Matched entries auto-action as Accept, while unmatched rows remain Pending for manual Pair or Unmatch overrides. Not-filed entries are ignored.

4

1-Click Export

Export portal-ready IMS Action JSON files for direct GST portal upload, along with formatted Excel spreadsheets and printable PDF reports.
