Simple Process
How GSTSyncPro Works

From file upload to ITC summary in four easy steps. No data leaves your browser.

1

Upload Files

Drag & drop your GSTR-2B/IMS Purchase, Books Purchase, GSTR-2B/IMS CN/DN, and Books CN/DN files directly. Commas in amounts are automatically removed for error-free parsing.

2

Auto-Reconcile

GSTSyncPro runs 3-pass purchase matching (Exact → Fuzzy → Amount) and 6-pass CN/DN matching. Toggle between GSTR-2B and IMS modes instantly.

3

Review & Act

Accept, unmatch, pair, force-pair or mark as "Not Matched" on each record. Filter by IMS status (Pending, Accepted, Rejected) or supplier GSTIN.

4

Export Results

Export your reconciliation as formatted Excel, IMS JSON (upload-ready), or copy the ITC summary directly — Net Claimable ready.
