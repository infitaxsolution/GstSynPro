Everything you need to know about GSTSyncPro, 2-Files Mode as-is portal upload, 4-Files Mode template headers, side-by-side matching, and privacy.

The Side-by-Side view is specifically designed for reviewing and manually pairing non-matched / pending transactions. Matched entries are automatically assigned Accept status, while unmatched transactions remain Pending in the side-by-side table so you can perform manual Pair, Force Pair, or Unmatch overrides.
GST Portal files (GSTR-2B or IMS downloads) are uploaded completely as-is without editing ONLY in 2-Files Mode. In 4-Files Mode, standard template headers are required for both Portal and Books files.
Your Books purchase file should contain standard Tally or ERP column headers (such as Supplier GSTIN, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, Total Invoice Value). You can also use our standard template format.
2-Files Mode allows direct as-is GST Portal file uploads paired with 1 Books purchase file. 4-Files Mode allows separate Purchase and Credit/Debit Note files using standard template column headers for both Portal and Books.
Pair matches entries with identical or matching values. Force Pair allows you to manually force a match between non-matched entries even if minor invoice number, date, or amount discrepancies exist.
